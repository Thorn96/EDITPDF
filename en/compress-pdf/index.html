Free · no sign-up · the file stays on your device

In 3 steps

  1. Open your PDFDrop it on the Rature page or click “Choose a file”. It opens in your browser: nothing is uploaded.
  2. Tick “Reduce the file size”Click “Download”, then tick “Reduce the file size”. From this guide, the box is already ticked.
  3. DownloadThe lighter PDF is saved to your device. Your original file is not changed.

What gets lighter

Photos and scans are recompressed, and the file is cleaned of unnecessary data. The text stays sharp: it is not turned into an image.

Still too heavy?

Delete the pages you don’t need (grid view, G key), or download only the pages that are required with “Only”.

Scanning a document?

When scanning, choose “Black and white”: it’s the lightest result, and the most readable for text.

Questions

How much smaller will the file be?

It depends on the content: a PDF full of photos gets much lighter, a PDF made mostly of text is often already light.

Does the quality drop?

Photos are slightly less detailed, with no visible difference on screen in most cases. The text stays identical.

Is my document sent over the Internet?

No. Everything happens in your browser: the file never leaves your device, and Rature even works offline once opened.

Is it really free?

Yes: no sign-up, no document limit, and no watermark added.
