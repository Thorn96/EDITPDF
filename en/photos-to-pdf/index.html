In 3 steps

  1. Pick your photosClick “Choose a file” and select one or more images. Each image becomes an A4 page, in its orientation.
  2. Arrange and completePut the pages in order, add text or your signature if needed.
  3. DownloadOne PDF, ready to send.

A sheet taken as a photo? Scan it

“Scan a document” opens your phone’s camera: place the 4 corners on the edges of the sheet, Rature straightens it and turns it black and white, like a scanner.

Make the photo’s text editable

A photo of a document only contains an image. The “OCR” button reads its text: you can then fix it, search it or copy it.

Add photos to an existing PDF

A document is already open? “PDF or images”, in the pages panel, adds the photos as new pages, or places them on the current page.
