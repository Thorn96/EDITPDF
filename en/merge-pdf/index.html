Free · no sign-up · the file stays on your device

In 3 steps

  1. Open all your filesSelect several PDFs at once (Ctrl or Shift + click), or drop them together on the page. Photos work too: they become pages.
  2. Arrange the pagesIn the pages panel, drag a page to move it. The grid view (G key) shows the whole document: rotate, duplicate, delete.
  3. DownloadClick “Download”: one PDF with all your pages.

Add a file along the way

A document is already open? “PDF or images”, at the bottom of the pages panel, appends more files. You can also drop one on the page: Rature asks whether to replace, append or open a new tab.

Keep only some pages

When downloading, choose “Only” and enter the pages you want (for example 1-3, 5). To do the opposite and split a PDF into several files: “Split the PDF”, in “More tools”.

Everything stays on your device

The files are never uploaded: handy for combining sensitive documents, such as a rental application or proofs of income.

Questions

How many files can I merge?

As many as you like: the only limit is your device’s memory.

Can I mix PDFs and photos?

Yes: each photo becomes a page, in the image’s orientation.

What about a Word document?

.docx files are converted to PDF when opened, then combined with the others.

Is it really free?

Yes: no sign-up, no document limit, and no watermark added.
