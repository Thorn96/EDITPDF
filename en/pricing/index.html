Does the free version change?

No. Every editor feature stays free, with no sign-up and no watermark. Without an account, your files never leave your device.

Who can read my synced documents?

Nobody but you. They are encrypted in your browser before they are sent, with a key that never leaves your devices. To use your account on a new computer, you scan a QR code with a device already signed in, or type your recovery phrase.
