Convert a PDF to Excel

Get the tables of a PDF into an Excel file: every column in its place, and numbers you can add up straight away.

Free · no sign-up · the file stays on your device

In 3 steps

  1. Open your PDF or photoDrop it on this page or click “Convert my PDF”. A photo of a table (JPG, PNG) works too. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Export to ExcelIn “Tools”, choose “PDF to Excel (.xlsx)”. From this guide, an “Export to Excel” button appears as soon as the file opens.
  3. Open it in ExcelThe .xlsx file opens in Excel, LibreOffice Calc or Google Sheets: one sheet per page of the PDF.

Numbers, not text

Amounts (“1,250.50”, “€45”), percentages and numbers become real Excel numbers, with a currency or percentage format: you can add them up straight away. Phone numbers, postcodes and account numbers stay text, so nothing gets mangled.

A photo or scan of a table

Open the photo or the scanned PDF: Rature first reads its text (OCR), automatically, then puts every word in its column according to where it sits on the page.

How the columns are found

A table with borders is taken cell by cell. Without borders, the gaps between the columns separate them: a right-aligned amount still lands in the right column.

Questions

What about a PDF with several pages?

Each page becomes a sheet of the workbook, named “Page 1”, “Page 2”…

And the other way round?

Open an Excel workbook (.xlsx) in Rature: it is converted to PDF. See the Excel to PDF guide.

Is my document sent over the Internet?

No. Everything happens in your browser: the file never leaves your device, and Rature even works offline once opened.

Is it really free?

Yes: no sign-up, no document limit, and no watermark added.
