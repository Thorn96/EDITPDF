In 3 steps

  1. Open your Excel fileDrop the .xlsx file on this page or click “Choose my Excel file”. It is converted to PDF on your device.
  2. Fill in if neededAdd a signature, a stamp or some text, as on any PDF.
  3. Download the PDFClick “Download”: your PDF is ready to send.

What is kept

Values as Excel displays them (currency amounts, percentages, dates), bold, italics, text and cell colours, borders, alignment, wrapped text and merged cells. Hidden sheets are left out.

A table that is too wide?

A wide sheet switches to landscape, then shrinks to fit the width of the page: no column is cut off onto another page.

Several sheets

Each visible sheet of the workbook starts on a new page, in the order of the tabs.

Questions

Are formulas calculated?

Rature uses the result saved by Excel. A file made by another program without saved results shows empty cells: open it and save it once in Excel or LibreOffice.

What about charts?

They are not carried over: only the cells of the table are.

Is my document sent over the Internet?

No. Everything happens in your browser: the file never leaves your device, and Rature even works offline once opened.

Is it really free?

Yes: no sign-up, no document limit, and no watermark added.
