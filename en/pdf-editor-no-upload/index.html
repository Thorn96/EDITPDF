Why it matters

Many online PDF services process files on their servers, even if they delete them afterwards. For an ID card, a tax notice or a contract, you’d probably rather they went nowhere. With Rature, they go nowhere.

How to check

Open Rature, then turn off your Internet connection: everything keeps working. And the code is public: anyone can check what it does.

Questions

How can Rature be free?

The site costs almost nothing to run, since your device does the work. If Rature helps you, you can buy the developer a coffee.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You can still install it as an app (the “Install” button) to open it offline.

Is there any visitor tracking?

Only an anonymous visit count, without cookies. The content of your PDFs is never involved.

What about large files?

Rature only draws the pages on screen: large documents stay smooth. The only limit is your device’s memory.
