Convert a PDF to Word

Get the text of a PDF into a Word file, with its paragraphs, headings, bold and italics.

Convert my PDF →

Free · no sign-up · the file stays on your device

In 3 steps

  1. Open your PDFDrop it on the Rature page or click “Choose a file”. It opens in your browser: nothing is uploaded.
  2. Export to WordIn “More tools”, choose “Export to Word (.docx)”. From this guide, an “Export to Word” button appears as soon as the file opens.
  3. Open it in WordThe .docx file opens in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs.

What is kept

The text, its paragraphs, headings, bold, italics and font size. Images, complex tables and column layouts are not carried over: to keep the exact look, edit the PDF directly with “Edit text” instead.

A scanned PDF?

Run the “OCR” button first: the recognised text then goes into the Word file.

And the other way round?

Open a Word document (.docx) in Rature: it is converted to PDF, ready to be filled in, signed or sent.

Questions

Are my edits included?

Yes: the Word file contains the document with your changes.

Is my document sent over the Internet?

No. Everything happens in your browser: the file never leaves your device, and Rature even works offline once opened.

Is it really free?

Yes: no sign-up, no document limit, and no watermark added.
