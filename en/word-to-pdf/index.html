In 3 steps

  1. Open your Word documentDrop the .docx file on this page or click “Choose my Word file”. The conversion happens on your device.
  2. Check and fill inThe document shows page by page. Add a signature, or fix a line with “Edit text”.
  3. Download the PDFClick “Download”: your PDF is ready to send.

What is kept

The document’s styles (headings, paragraphs, line spacing), bulleted and numbered lists, tabs, tables with their borders, shading and merged cells, images (even those placed in front of the text), headers and footers with the page number, columns and links.

Fonts

Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman and Courier New are replaced by free fonts with the same widths: the text falls in the same places as in Word. With Cambria or Aptos, a line may break one word earlier or later.

Several documents in one PDF

Choose several Word or PDF files at once: they are combined into a single PDF, which you can reorder before downloading.

Questions

What about old .doc files?

Only the .docx format (Word 2007 and later) is read. Open the .doc in Word or LibreOffice and save it as .docx.

And the other way round?

See the PDF to Word guide.

Is my document sent over the Internet?

No. Everything happens in your browser: the file never leaves your device, and Rature even works offline once opened.

Is it really free?

Yes: no sign-up, no document limit, and no watermark added.
